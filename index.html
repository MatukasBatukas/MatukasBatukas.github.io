Biography

Eight Years, One Vision

My photography began where most things do — with obsessive curiosity and a camera I could barely afford. Over eight years I've developed a practice that sits at the intersection of documentary instinct and editorial precision.

Based in London, working worldwide. I collaborate with brands, individuals, and production companies who understand that great visual work isn't made quickly — it's made carefully.

Questions Answered

Frequently Asked

Start by submitting an inquiry through the booking page. I'll respond within 24–48 hours to discuss your project, timeline, and requirements. Once we align on scope and price, I send a contract and invoice for a deposit (typically 25–50%) to secure your date.

Every project is different, but you can find starting prices on the Services page. Rates depend on duration, location, deliverables, licensing, and turnaround time. Commercial and brand projects include usage licensing. I provide detailed written quotes — no hidden costs.

Yes. I'm based in London but regularly travel across the UK and internationally. Travel costs (flights, accommodation) are charged at cost and outlined in the quote. For destination weddings and international brand projects, I've worked in Europe, the Middle East, and the US.

Portrait sessions: 5–10 working days. Weddings: 4–6 weeks for photos, 6–10 weeks for films. Commercial work: agreed in contract, typically 1–2 weeks. Rush delivery (within 72 hours) is available for an additional fee.

I shoot on Sony A7R V and FX3 systems, with a full range of G-Master lenses. Lighting is handled with Profoto B10 and Aputure LED setups. Cinema work uses the DJI Ronin gimbal system and DJI Mavic 3 Pro drone. Full gear list is on the About page.

All images are delivered via a private, password-protected online gallery (hosted for 12 months) from which you can download full-resolution files. Videos are delivered via Vimeo private links or Google Drive. Physical USBs can be arranged on request.

I don't deliver raw files as standard — the editing and color grading is a fundamental part of the finished work. However, for commercial clients with specific requirements, this can be discussed and negotiated in the contract.

Deposits are non-refundable but are transferable to a rescheduled date within 12 months. Cancellations within 14 days of the shoot date require the full balance. Full details are outlined in the booking contract.
