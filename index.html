Biography

19 Years Old, Camera in Hand

I'm Matas — online as Matukas — a 19-year-old studying BA (Hons) Film, Cinema & Video Studies at Ravensbourne University London. Before that: Level 3 Film & TV and two years of further study at South Essex College, plus a placement on Channel 4's 4Skills work experience programme.

Outside of coursework I shoot street, automotive, and food photography around London, direct short films under the name Matukas Films, and — when deadlines allow — build and film cinematic Minecraft worlds. Professional gear? Not yet. A good eye, a laptop, and too much coffee? Always.
